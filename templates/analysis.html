Methodology

How the model was trained and evaluated

Held-out evaluation

Performance metrics on this page come from a dedicated evaluation split that was never used during training — so the numbers reflect real generalization, not memorization.

Signal-focused feature pipeline

The engine prioritizes the most informative connection attributes before classification, reducing noise and keeping inference focused on what matters for detection quality.

Balanced learning for rare attack families

Rare but high-impact attack categories receive corrective weighting during training so they are not drowned out by majority traffic patterns.

Ensemble decision layer

Multiple classifiers contribute probability-weighted votes, producing more stable and accurate outcomes than any single model operating alone.
